Your electronic ID. Your documents. A straightforward signing workflow.

GET STARTED
1. Choose a document or drop it onto Sign.
2. Choose NFC or a compatible card reader.
3. Continue, confirm your PIN, then hold the card near your phone until signing finishes. With a reader, leave the card inserted.
4. Verify, save or share the signed document.

Sign PDFs with PAdES and other files with XAdES. Verify signatures and view certificate details. Manage cards and saved PINs in Settings. The same card identity and saved PIN work with NFC and a reader. Choose Romanian, English or Russian and a light or dark appearance. Compatible portals are available in the built-in browser; NFC document signing does not imply NFC login support for every portal.

You need a supported Moldovan electronic identity card with an active signing certificate and its PIN. NFC signing requires a compatible contactless card and an NFC-capable device. Card generations and devices can differ; a compatible external reader remains available. Internet access is needed for timestamps, online certificate checks and portals.

The private key stays on your card. An incorrect PIN is never retried automatically. Demo mode uses fictional data and does not create real signatures.
