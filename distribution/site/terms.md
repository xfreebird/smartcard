# eSemnBun usage information

Draft content for https://esemnbun.md/terms — owner review required before publication. This is operational information, not a finalized legal agreement.

eSemnBun is an independent document-signing utility. It does not represent the Government of Moldova or the linked public-service operators. Third-party sites and trust services have their own availability, authentication requirements and terms.

You control which documents and signing requests you authorize. Review the selected identity, requested operation and destination before approval. Keep your card and PIN secure. Demo-mode results are previews and are not real signatures.

A compatible reader, active electronic ID card and valid signing certificate are required for real signing. NFC is unsupported. The app cannot guarantee acceptance of every file, signature format or administrative submission by every recipient.
