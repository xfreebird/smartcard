# eSemnBun support

Draft content for https://esemnbun.md/support — not published.

Author: Nicolae Ghimbovschi. Contact: nicolae.ghimbovschi@gmail.com.

To report a problem, include the app version, operating-system version, reader model and the steps that failed. Do not send your PIN, private keys, complete identity-card data or confidential documents. Use a synthetic sample if a document is needed to reproduce an issue.

Start by connecting a compatible reader and inserting an active Moldovan electronic ID card. Select the intended identity, add documents and approve the native request. A suitable USB-C, Lightning or Android OTG adapter may be needed. NFC is not supported.

If the PIN is rejected, stop and check the correct card/PIN. Do not repeatedly guess. Use official card-provider support for a blocked card. If another signing application owns the local ports, close it or use the confirmed reconnect action where available.

The embedded browser has service shortcuts and controls for clearing cache, cookies/site data and history. Clearing cookies signs you out of sites; it does not delete saved PINs or documents.

Owner publication tasks: add a maintained compatibility list, troubleshooting pages and response expectations. Do not publish promises of hardware compatibility or guaranteed response times without confirming them.
