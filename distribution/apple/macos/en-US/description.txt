Your electronic ID. Your documents. A straightforward signing workflow.

eSemnBun helps you sign PDFs and other files using the certificate on your Moldovan electronic identity card.

GET STARTED
1. Connect a compatible reader and insert your card.
2. Choose your card and add documents.
3. Approve the signing request and authorize PIN use.
4. Verify the result, then save or share your signed files.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Sign PDFs with PAdES and other files with XAdES.
• Prepare a batch of documents and authorize that batch once.
• Verify signatures and inspect certificate details.
• Use the built-in browser for card signing and login on compatible services.
• Open MSign, EVO, eVMS and MPay from service shortcuts.
• Choose Romanian, English or Russian and a light or dark appearance.
• Manage saved PINs and separately clear browser cache, cookies or history.

REQUIREMENTS
You need an active Moldovan electronic identity card, a valid signing certificate and a compatible card reader. Use an appropriate adapter for your device when needed. NFC is not supported. Internet access is needed for portals, timestamps and online certificate checks.

Your private key stays on the card. An incorrect PIN is never retried automatically. Demo mode lets you explore the interface without hardware; its outputs are not real signatures.

eSemnBun is an independent app. It does not represent the Government of Moldova or public-service operators. Portal availability and authentication methods are controlled by their providers.

The Mac App Store version uses CryptoTokenKit and App Sandbox. Export results to a chosen location. The external card-insertion helper, vendor-app process control and browser routes accepting arbitrary filesystem paths are not included.
