# A little help, when you need it

Start with the [setup guide](/docs/start) or [troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting).

For bugs and feature requests, [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/xfreebird/smartcard/issues). Include the app version, operating-system version, reader model and steps that failed.

For private support questions, contact **Nicolae Ghimbovschi** at [nicolae.ghimbovschi@gmail.com](mailto:nicolae.ghimbovschi@gmail.com).

Never send your card PIN, private keys, full identity-card data or confidential documents. Use a synthetic sample when reporting a document problem. This community project does not promise a fixed response time.

For a blocked card or forgotten PIN, contact your official card provider. eSemnBun cannot unblock a card.
