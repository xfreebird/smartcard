# Use your ID on supported websites

The app provides shortcuts to services such as MSign and EVO. These services are operated independently and have their own terms and availability.

On supported mobile devices, use the in-app browser or the external-browser connection where available. The in-app browser includes URL navigation, back and forward controls, and data-clearing options. Some identity login pages require desktop-site presentation.

The Mac App Store version uses a sandboxed browser workflow and has different integration capabilities from the full desktop version. Choose the workflow offered by your installed version.

Check the website address, selected identity and signing request before authorizing. Clearing browser cookies can sign you out; it does not delete your documents or saved card PINs.
