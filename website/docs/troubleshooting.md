# When something does not work

## Sign over NFC

If the NFC session is interrupted, keep the card near the antenna until the scan closes. Confirm the selected card and PIN before retrying a failed PIN entry. You can switch to a compatible USB reader in Sign.

## Reader or card is missing

Reconnect the reader, check the adapter and reinsert the card. Try a direct connection rather than a hub. Confirm the operating system recognizes your reader. Desktop apps may offer a configurable insertion helper.

## Incorrect PIN

Stop and check which card is selected. Do not repeatedly guess: the card can lock after incorrect attempts. A blocked card requires help from the official card provider.

## Browser connection is unavailable

Another signing application may already own the local connection port. Close the competing app, then reconnect using eSemnBun's browser settings. If the browser requests local-network permission, review it for the site you intend to use.

## Report a problem

Include your app and OS versions, reader model, and steps to reproduce. Use fictional documents for examples. Do not attach PINs, identity-card photographs, private keys or confidential files. Visit [Support](/support).
