eSemnBun Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026
Developer: Nicolae Ghimbovschi, Republic of Moldova
Contact: nicolae.ghimbovschi@gmail.com

eSemnBun lets you sign and verify documents with the qualified certificate on your Moldovan electronic identity card,
on macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS and Android. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes.


SUMMARY

- eSemnBun has no user accounts, advertising or cross-app tracking. The desktop apps offer optional Sentry crash reporting and application metrics. iOS, Android and command-line tools do not include Sentry.
- Your documents never leave your device. Signing happens on your device and on your ID card.
- Desktop diagnostics are off by default. The first launch asks separately about crash reports and application metrics. Nothing is sent to Sentry until you opt in. You can change either choice in Settings.
- To make signatures legally valid, the app contacts Moldova's public trust services (timestamping and
  certificate-status checks). These requests contain hashes and certificate identifiers, never your documents.


INFORMATION THE APP PROCESSES ON YOUR DEVICE

- The certificate on your ID card (your name, certificate number, issuer, validity): read from the card to show which
  card is inserted and to create signatures. It is not sent to the developer.
- Your PIN: sent only to your ID card, over the card's encrypted channel, to unlock the signing key. If you choose
  "remember PIN", it is kept in your operating system's secure credential store (Apple Keychain, Android Keystore,
  Windows Credential Locker or GNOME Keyring), protected by your device lock or biometrics where the platform supports
  this, and tied to that card. You can delete it at any time in the app.
- Documents you sign or verify: processed locally to compute and check signatures. Signed copies are saved next to the
  original (or in the app's folder on iOS).
- Activity list (file names, dates, results): stored only on your device so you can find recent documents. You can
  clear it at any time.
- Settings: stored only on your device.

Biometric checks (Face ID, Touch ID, Windows Hello) are performed by your operating system; the app never receives
biometric data.


INFORMATION SENT OVER THE NETWORK

The app connects to the following services:

- Timestamp authority (tsp.pki.gov.md, Moldovan public-key infrastructure): when a signature includes a timestamp,
  the app sends a cryptographic hash (SHA-256) of the signature value. The document and its contents are not sent.
- Certificate status (OCSP) and revocation lists (CRL) (mdqsign.ocsp.pki.md, pki.md): for signature formats that embed
  validation data, the app asks whether a certificate is still valid. The OCSP request contains the certificate's
  serial number and identifiers of its issuer. Revocation lists are public files that are downloaded.
- Browser connection (optional): the app can run a local service on your own device (127.0.0.1, not reachable from
  other devices) so that government e-signature websites such as msign.gov.md can ask for a signature. Only websites
  on the official MoldSign allow-list are accepted. Before every signature the app shows which website is asking and
  what will be signed. The resulting signature is returned only to that website, after your confirmation. You can
  switch this feature off in the app.
- Optional desktop reliability diagnostics (Sentry): after opt-in, app errors, technical stack frames, operating-system and app version,
  build and environment, and application counters, duration metrics and sampled timings are sent to the developer's Sentry organization in the EU region, only for the categories you enabled.
  Document contents and names, PINs, cardholder names, certificate identifiers, browser URLs, screenshots,
  session recordings and arbitrary application logs are excluded by the application's diagnostic boundary.
  The receiving network service processes the connection IP address as part of delivering the report.
  These reports are used to find and fix failures, not for advertising or cross-app tracking. Disabling a category stops its reporting immediately and discards unsent local reports. It does not delete reports already received by Sentry.
- In-app browser (iOS): opens msign.gov.md so the browser connection works on iPhone and iPad. That website's own
  privacy policy applies to what you do there.

The public trust services are operated by Moldovan public institutions. Sentry operates the diagnostic service
on the developer's behalf. The respective providers' privacy policies apply to their processing.


WHAT WE DO NOT DO

- We do not sell personal information or use advertising identifiers.
- We do not record screens, document interactions or PIN entry. Diagnostic reports are limited as described above.
  Your operating system may separately send crash reports to Apple, Google or Microsoft under its own policies.
- We do not access your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.


PERMISSIONS

- USB / smart-card access: to talk to your card reader.
- Network: for timestamping, certificate-status checks, reliability diagnostics and the optional local browser connection.
- Notifications (Android): to show the status of the browser connection.
- Face ID / Touch ID / Windows Hello: only if you choose to remember your PIN, to unlock it.


DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

PINs, settings and the activity list are stored on your device. Deleting the remembered PIN, clearing the activity
list, or uninstalling the app removes that local data. Signed documents remain wherever you saved them.
If you opted in on a desktop app, diagnostic reports are stored separately in Sentry under the organization's configured retention period.
Uninstalling the app does not delete reports already sent. Contact the developer about diagnostic data requests.


CHILDREN

eSemnBun is a tool for adults who hold a Moldovan electronic identity card and is not directed at children.


YOUR RIGHTS

Contact nicolae.ghimbovschi@gmail.com about access, correction or deletion requests relating to diagnostics.
Questions about how the public trust services process requests should go to their operator. Moldova's personal data protection
law (Law No. 133/2011) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation apply to this policy.


CHANGES

If the app's handling of information changes, we will update this policy and its effective date before releasing that
version.
